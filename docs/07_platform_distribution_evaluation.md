## 1. Executive Summary: The Three-Tier Platform Architecture

Because physical ownership of broadcast magnetic media or a station hard drive transfers **chattel property** (`17 U.S.C. § 202`) rather than underlying KTXL/Nexstar or NBA broadcast copyright, distribution platforms divide into **three distinct operational tiers**:

1. **Tier 1 — High-ROI & Legally Safe Primary Channels (Execute Immediately):**
   - **Direct B2B Archival Screener Funnel ("The Periscope Films Model"):** Publishing timecoded, watermarked reference previews on YouTube + sending direct 15-second `59.94 fps` sample clips to documentary producers, sports historians, and true-crime creators to close private **"Archival Research, Digitization & Master-Tape Access Fees"** (`$150 – $1,500+` per deal).
   - **Transformative YouTube Long-Form (`8–18 mins`, `16:9` at `59.94 fps`):** Narrated mini-documentaries and visual breakdowns (`$4.00 – $15.00+` RPM) engineered to pass both YouTube's **Reused Content** policy and the **July 15, 2025 / July 16, 2026 Inauthentic Content** policy updates.
   - **TikTok Creator Rewards (`62–75 sec`, `9:16` at `59.94 fps`) + Facebook Content Monetization:** Transformative `60s+` vertical breakdowns (`$0.40 – $1.50+` RPM on TikTok; `$0.50 – $3.00` RPM on Facebook), paired with `20–45 sec` **YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels / X** viral teasers that funnel viewers into Long-Form videos and B2B inquiries.
   - **eBay Hardware Liquidation:** Selling the physical **Harmonic ProMedia Carbon USB Dongle** (`$250 – $500`) and **wiped 3 TB HGST Enterprise Hard Drive** (`$40 – $55`).
2. **Tier 2 — Community & Discovery Multipliers (Layer 2):**
   - **Reddit (`r/ObscureMedia`, `r/Sacramento`, `r/kings`, `r/chicagobulls`, `r/lostmedia`) + Internet Archive (`archive.org`):** Zero direct ad payout, but the #1 organic discovery engine for attracting documentary producers, journalists, and initial subscribers from day one.
   - **Ko-fi & Patreon ("Preservation & Digitization Tip Jar / Supporter Tier"):** Recurring fan support (`$5/mo` or one-off preservation tips) funding archival storage and behind-the-scenes restoration logs (never paywalling raw copyrighted broadcasts).
3. **Tier 3 — Disqualified / High-Risk Traps (Avoid Completely):**
   - **Self-Serve Stock Sites (Pond5, Getty, Shutterstock, Storyblocks)** and **AI Video Training Brokers (Motion56)** (require signed copyright chain-of-title warranties for the entire portfolio).
   - **Raw Unedited Clip Uploads on YouTube or `<60s` Reposts on TikTok** (earn `$0.00` due to YPP Reused Content rejection and TikTok's 60-second/originality gate).

## 3. Primary-Source Policy Analysis & Compliance Rules

### 3.1 Stock Marketplaces vs. The "Periscope Films" Archival Screener Model
* **Why Pond5, Getty, Shutterstock, and Motion56 Are Off-Limits:**
  - Official Pond5 contributor documentation ([Pond5 Archival Footage Rules](https://contributor.pond5.com/2022/04), [Pond5 Preparing Your Files](https://contributor.pond5.com/getting-started/preparing-your-files-2/footage)) mandates that contributors uploading vintage or archival footage sign a representation agreement proving **copyright ownership or verified public-domain chain of title for the entire portfolio**.
  - AI video licensing brokers such as **Motion56** ([Motion56 Content Owners](https://motion56.com/content-owners)) require `500+ hours` of footage and conduct chain-of-title verification prior to licensing to AI developers.
* **How the "Periscope Films" Screener Model Works Legally:**
  - Archival houses like **Periscope Films** ([Periscope Film Licensing Guide](https://periscopefilm.com/licensing-archival-footage)) and findings from the **BAVC Media Magnetic Media Study** ([Documentary.org / BAVC Study](https://documentary.org/feature/what-filmmakers-should-know-about-privacy-and-copyright-bavc-medias-upcoming-landmark-study)) and **Electronic Arts Intermix** ([EAI Best Practices](https://eai.org/resourceguide/collection/singlechannel/bestpractices.html)) demonstrate how physical collection holders monetize rare magnetic media:
    1. Upload **timecoded, watermarked reference previews** (or narrated documentary breakdowns) to YouTube with detailed metadata (dates, names, locations, tape format) in the video description.
    2. When a documentary producer, podcast network, or YouTuber finds the clip via Google/YouTube search and contacts you, you charge a private **Archival Research, Digitization & Master-File Transfer Fee** (`$150 – $1,500+`) for supplying the clean, uncompressed (`2vuy` / `ProRes 422`) master file, while the buyer clears their own **Editorial Fair Use** (`17 U.S.C. § 107`) or Errors & Omissions (E&O) insurance.

### 3.2 YouTube Partner Program (YPP): Passing "Reused Content" & "Inauthentic Content" (2025–2026 Rules)
Based on YouTube's official monetization policies ([YouTube Help Answer 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=EN)) and 2026 compliance audits ([KeepMonetized](https://keepmonetized.com/guides/reused-vs-inauthentic-content), [Umbrella Creators](https://umbrellacreators.com/blog/youtube-inauthentic-content-policy-edited-videos), [Binai](https://binai.it/en/blog/youtube-reused-content-policy), [Lume Studio](https://lume-studio.ai/blog/youtube-inauthentic-content-ai-channels)):

1. **Reused Content Compliance Checklist:**
   - **Rule:** Reused content is judged at the **channel level**, not per video, and applies even if you have permission.
   - **What Fails:** Uploading unedited `Untitled` reels, or stringing together clips with only background music or text overlays. Also avoid starting a video with more than `5–8 seconds` of uninterrupted raw clip before your voiceover hook begins ([r/PartneredYoutube Case Study](https://reddit.com/r/PartneredYoutube/comments/1xglte1/youtube_monetization_rejected_for_reused_content)).
   - **What Explicitly Passes:** YouTube's official policy explicitly lists *"Replays of a sports tournament where you explain the moves a competitor did to succeed"* and *"Edited footage from other creators where you add a storyline and commentary"* as monetizable.
2. **July 15, 2025 & July 16, 2026 "Inauthentic Content" Compliance Checklist:**
   - YouTube split "Inauthentic Content" into three categories on July 16, 2026: *Generic or Repetitive Content*, *Unsatisfying or Off-putting Content*, and *AI Personas Related to Sensitive Topics*.
   - **4-Point Self-Check Before Publishing Any Long-Form Video:**
     - [ ] **Unique Script Test:** Does the script cite specific visual details visible only on your master tape (e.g., camera angles, jersey numbers, street signs, ambient audio), rather than reading a Wikipedia summary aloud?
     - [ ] **5-to-8-Second Visual Rhythm Test:** Does the visual frame change every `5–8 seconds` (via freeze-frame zoom, telestrator circle, split-track audio callout, historical headline graphic, or camera cut) in sync with your narration?
     - [ ] **Human / Authentic Voiceover:** Record your own voiceover (even on a smartphone microphone with basic noise reduction) to build instant reviewer trust.
     - [ ] **Transparent Channel `About` Page:** Include a clear statement in your channel description: *"California Broadcast Vault restores, deinterlaces (486i to 59.94 fps), and produces historical video essays and technical breakdowns from preserved Northern California Betacam SP and DVCPro broadcast master tapes."*

### 3.3 TikTok Creator Rewards vs. Short-Form Vertical Platforms (2026 Rules)
Cross-checked against 2026 Creator Rewards studies ([ShortSync 2026 Guide](https://shortsync.app/resources/tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026), [FindClout YPP vs. Clipping](https://findclout.com/blog/clipping-vs-youtube-partner-program), [Neal Schaffer 2026 Breakdown](https://nealschaffer.com/tiktok-creator-rewards-program), [FlowShorts Data](https://flowshorts.app/blog/tiktok-pay-per-view)):
* **The 60-Second Rule:** Any TikTok video `59 seconds` or shorter earns **`$0.00`** from Creator Rewards.
* **The Originality Filter:** Unedited TV clips cropped to `9:16` are disqualified by TikTok's automated originality check.
* **Dual-Length Vertical Execution:**
  - **For TikTok Creator Rewards & Facebook Reels (`62–75 seconds`):** Package 3 related moments or a multi-step story with an immediate 3-second voiceover hook, dynamic zoom-ins, and burned-in captions.
  - **For YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels & X (`20–45 seconds`):** Cut single high-impact moments (e.g., the 36-second 1989 Michael Jordan courtside sequence) to maximize loop completion (`>85%`) and drive viewers to your YouTube Long-Form link and B2B DM inbox.
